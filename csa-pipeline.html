Why two branches?
CCompi = a(block i) + b(block i−1)

a summarizes the current block while b carries in the previous block. Their overlap keeps information continuous across block boundaries; they are not separate key and value branches.

Fixed toy setup: n = 16, d = 8, c = 6, dc = 4, cI = 4, m = 4, top-k = 2, sliding window = 2 tokens, 4 indexer heads, 8 attention heads, 2 output groups, dg = 8.
